Free GTO Solver — right in your browser

A free GTO solver for Texas Hold'em postflop strategy. No payment, no sign-up, no installation. Enter your ranges, a board, and bet sizes — everything is computed on your own device, and nothing you solve is sent to a server.

The solver will start automatically in a moment. If the screen doesn't change, please enable JavaScript in your browser.

What you can do

  • Postflop GTO solving — flop, turn, and river, with your own bet and raise sizes
  • Preflop charts — opening and defense ranges by position, plus vs-3bet and vs-4bet modes
  • Equity calculator — hand-vs-hand and hand-vs-range equity with hand breakdowns
  • Spot share links — share any spot you build with a single URL
  • 12 languages — English, 한국어, 日本語, Español, Português, Deutsch, Français, Bahasa Indonesia, Bahasa Melayu, हिन्दी, 简体中文, 繁體中文

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — every feature, with no usage limits. No payment method or account is ever required.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser, and solving uses your own CPU. Desktop Chrome is recommended for big spots.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. On iPhone/Safari solving is slower due to browser limits, so we recommend a PC for custom solves — the preflop charts and equity calculator work great on any phone.
